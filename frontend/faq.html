Typing Japanese

How do I type kana with a non-Japanese keyboard?

In fields that support romaji conversion, type the sounds using Latin letters: ka becomes か, and kya becomes きゃ. The expected script depends on the tool and question. A Japanese keyboard or input method (IME) can also be used.

For ん, try nn. A doubled consonant can produce a small っ, as in kitte → きって. Exact conversion and confirmation can vary by input method.

How do I type a small kana by itself?

In Pazuru's Clue popup or another compatible Japanese input, prefix the kana with x or l:

  • xya or lya → ゃ
  • xyu or lyu → ゅ
  • xyo or lyo → ょ
  • xtsu or ltsu → っ
  • xa, xi, xu, xe, xo → ぁ ぃ ぅ ぇ ぉ

If a single In-square cell in Pazuru does not accept the shortcut, try a Japanese IME or switch Grid input style to Clue popup and enter the whole answer. Shortcuts can differ between keyboards and tools.

Why is my answer not being accepted?

Check the requested script and direction, including small kana and long vowels. Some questions ask for a reading; others ask for a meaning or written form. In Pazuru, Clue popup expects the complete answer, including any solved crossing letters.

Studying

How do I show furigana or romaji, or hear audio?

Use the shared controls in the tool's header when available. They apply across supported tools. Some controls are hidden where they would give away an answer or do not apply.

Why are some settings unavailable?

A setting may not apply to the selected mode or another choice. Its explanation appears beside the disabled control. Your saved choice is kept for when it becomes relevant again.

Are JLPT levels official?

Modern vocabulary levels are estimates, not official JLPT classifications. See Data and licenses for source details.

Using the site

How do I change the theme?

Use the appearance controls in the header to choose a palette and light, dark or system mode. Your preference is stored in this browser.

Where are my settings saved?

Supported preferences are stored in your browser. Clearing site data, using another browser or device, or blocking storage can prevent them from carrying over.

How can I report a problem or suggest an improvement?

Use the contact page and include the tool, what you tried, and what happened. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
