# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone visiting the site can use its pages to learn and practice Japanese.

## Product Purpose

NihongoToolkit is an online collection of interactive Japanese learning tools.
It should make learning and practice fun, remain free to use, and support
installation as a progressive web app. Paid content is planned.

## Operating Context

The site is deployed at https://nihongotoolkit.pages.dev/. It includes practice,
reference, lookup, games, and printable study resources. Supported settings and
progress are stored in the browser rather than an account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing backend API hooks, form handlers, and data bindings.
- Preserve multiple color themes and light and dark modes.
- Support enabling or disabling romaji, furigana, and audio where applicable.
- Support responsive experiences, including mobile interaction behavior as
  well as visual layout.
- Preserve existing local preferences and the intended behavior of the tools.

## Brand Commitments

The product is called NihongoToolkit.

## Product Principles

- Make Japanese learning and practice interactive and enjoyable.
- Keep the collection accessible to anyone visiting the site.
- Respect learners' display and audio preferences.
- Make tools usable on mobile as well as desktop.
